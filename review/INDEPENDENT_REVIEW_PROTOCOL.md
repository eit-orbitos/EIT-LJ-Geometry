# Independent Review Protocol

The goal is adversarial verification, not agreement.

Each reviewer should independently check:
1. Algebraic derivation of the generalized equilibrium.
2. Definition and dimensional/normalization assumptions of the geometry factor.
3. Simplex cancellation claim.
4. Polygon extrema and continuum-limit numerics.
5. Platonic-solid vertex sets and radial projections.
6. Cuboid deformation and the effect of normalization choice.
7. Whether any result is being overinterpreted as physical validation.
8. Whether the 0.9/1.3 thresholds are correctly labeled heuristic.

Review outcomes should be recorded as: CONFIRMED, CORRECTED, UNRESOLVED, or REJECTED, with exact equations/code lines cited.
